    Towers

    • Turret — high single-target DPS, short range, hits air & ground.
    • Mortar — big splash, slow reload, long reach, cannot hit flying.
    • Coil — slows, strong vs air, weak vs heavy armor.

    Enemies & resists

    • Drone (flying) — resists Earth · weak to Wind / Water.
    • Crawler — slight Fire weakness · mild Wind resist.
    • Armor (heavy) — resists Fire / Wind · weak to Earth.
    • Swarm — resists Water · weak to Fire.
    • Behemoth — bulk boss; resists Fire / Water / Wind · weak to Earth.
    • Emberwyrm (flying) — ash regen unless chilled; resists Fire · weak to Water.
    • Shellwarden — regenerating carapace shield; resists Fire / Water / Wind · weak to Earth.
    • Broodmother — spawns Swarm adds; resists Water · weak to Fire.
    • Galeheart — Tempest enrage at low HP; resists Wind · weak to Fire / Earth.

    Boss types unlock across endless decades; multi-boss packs prefer mixed types. Square pips = resist; triangles = weak. Waves advance on a timer — overlaps OK.

    Elements (socket up to 2)

    • Fire — burn DoT.
    • Water — chill slow.
    • Earth — bonus vs armor + shred.
    • Wind — bonus vs flying + shove.

    Combos

    • Fire+Water → Steam burst (small AoE).
    • Fire+Earth → Magma (explode on kill).
    • Fire+Wind → Wildfire (burn spreads).
    • Water+Earth → Mud (heavy slow).
    • Water+Wind → Storm (shatter chilled foes).
    • Earth+Wind → Tempest (full dmg to air & armor).
